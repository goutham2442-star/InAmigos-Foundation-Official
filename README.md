# InAmigos Foundation - Official Webpage 🌟

Welcome to the official web platform of the **InAmigos Foundation**, a Section 8 registered non-profit organization licensed by the Central Government of India, headquartered in Chhattisgarh.

![InAmigos Impact](https://inamigosfoundation.org.in/public/storage/gallery/1743051412.jpg)

## 📖 About Us

Founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO), the InAmigos Foundation operates with a legacy of transparency, compassion, and grassroots change. Our massive network of student volunteers and interns leads on-ground campaigns across 28 states in India.

### 🏆 Credentials & Recognitions
- **Section 8 Registered** – Licensed by the Central Government
- **80G & 12A Certified** – Ensuring full tax-exempt benefits for donors
- **CSR-1 Registered** – Authorized for corporate partnerships
- **NITI Aayog Registered** – Aligned with national development goals
- **IAF ISO 9001:2015 Certified** – Committed to high-quality operational standards

## 🚀 Our Major Initiatives

We run six flagship projects to create real, measurable impact:

1. **Project Udaan** - Women's empowerment through skill development and education.
2. **Project SEVA** - Hunger relief and essential food distribution.
3. **Project Jeev** - Animal welfare, shelter, and medical care for strays.
4. **Project Prakriti** - Environmental conservation and large-scale tree plantation.
5. **Project Bachpanshala** - Quality education for children in rural areas.
6. **Project Vikas** - Youth skill development and leadership training.

## 💻 Tech Stack

This repository contains the premium, modern frontend for the foundation, built for extreme performance, smooth animations, and maximum donor conversion.

- **Framework:** React + Vite
- **Styling:** Vanilla CSS (CSS Modules) + Glassmorphism UI
- **Animations:** Framer Motion for buttery-smooth scrolling effects
- **Icons:** Lucide React
